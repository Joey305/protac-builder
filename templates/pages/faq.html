Frequently Asked Questions

Does PROTAC Builder guarantee that a generated molecule will degrade the target?

No. The builder standardizes components and assembles candidate degraders, but degradation still depends on broader structural, biophysical, cellular, and experimental factors.

Is this site a complete library of all warheads, linkers, or recruiters?

No. The site provides component hubs and assembly workflows, while deeper component-specific exploration may live in Warhead Hunter, E3 Ligandalyzer, or other connected resources.

When should I use Warhead Hunter or E3 Ligandalyzer first?

Use Warhead Hunter when the target-binding ligand is still under exploration, and use E3 Ligandalyzer when recruiter, scaffold, or ligase-side attachment context is still under exploration.

Can I use PROTAC Builder in scripted or batch workflows?

Yes. The site exposes API-oriented routes and an API Builder page to help with notebook, server, and batch workflows.
