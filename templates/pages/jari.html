Ternary-Complex Analysis

JARI-PROTACS

JARI — Joint Analysis for Reproducible Interface clustering — turns folders of PRosettaC-derived ternary-complex models into a reviewable similarity dataset, structural clusters, UMAP view, and hierarchical dendrogram.

It is the “what patterns survive across my model sets?” step after ternary modeling. JARI does not prove that a candidate degrades a protein; it makes pose-family comparison and reporting more reproducible.

The workflow
  1. Start with PRosettaC model folders organized by ligand, linker, design, or pose family.
  2. Compare models within and across those folders with DockQ.
  3. Aggregate and average structural similarity scores.
  4. Cluster models using distance = 1 − Average_DockQ.
  5. Review clusters and figures alongside chemistry, biology, and experimental data.

Where JARI fits in PROTAC Builder

1 · Assemble

Use PROTAC Builder to define a candidate from a POI warhead, linker, and E3 recruiter, preserving attachment-vector context.

2 · Model

Generate ternary-complex hypotheses with PRosettaC or another appropriate structure-based workflow.

3 · Compare

Use JARI to quantify whether model families are structurally similar, distinct, or internally inconsistent.

4 · Decide

Use cluster membership as a prioritization signal, then validate promising designs experimentally.

Keep the boundary clear: JARI analyzes structural similarity among model outputs. It is not a degradation predictor and it cannot replace cellular degradation experiments.
